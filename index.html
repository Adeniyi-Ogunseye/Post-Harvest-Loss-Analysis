Project Title One

Understanding the importance of post-harvest losses – and specifically why addressing and reducing them matters – is critical for global food security, economic stability, and resource conservation. This project sees to quantify, visualize, and map the economic and volume losses of key staple grains in Nigeria in order to identify which regions, crops, and supply chain stages experience the highest inefficiencies to help policymakers target interventions. SQL and Power BI were used for the analysis.
